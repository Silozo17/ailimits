Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026. This policy covers the AI Limits apps for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Windows.

The short version

We don't collect any data. AI Limits has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. Your sign-ins and usage numbers stay on your devices.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, such as which AIs and stats to show and the theme.
  • Your latest usage numbers, cached so the widget can show them straight away.
  • Your claude.ai session key, if you add one. It's stored in your Mac's Keychain (Windows: Credential Manager).
  • Access to your Codex folder, if you allow it, so the app can read the Codex app's sign-in and its usage logs.

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it, apart from the Keychain item, which you can remove first in Settings.

Who the app talks to

To show your usage, the app sends requests with your own sign-in directly to the AI provider:

  • Anthropic (claude.ai, api.anthropic.com) for Claude
  • OpenAI (chatgpt.com) for ChatGPT and Codex

Those requests fetch only your usage figures, never your conversations. They're covered by each provider's own privacy policy. The app identifies itself to Anthropic's API and to OpenAI as "AILimits". Requests to claude.ai use a standard browser identifier.

iCloud (Apple devices)

The Mac App Store version can share your usage numbers with the AI Limits iPhone app through your private iCloud storage. Only the numbers are shared, never sign-ins or session keys. The data is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we can't see it. You can turn this off in Settings → General.

Children

AI Limits isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? See the support page.

AI Limits is an independent app, not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI or Google.
