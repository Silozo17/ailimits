Getting started

Where's the app?

AI Limits lives in the menu bar (look for two percentages) and as a widget on your desktop. Click the menu bar item for Settings, or right-click the widget.

Connecting Claude

Open claude.ai in your browser, then open Developer Tools → Storage (Safari) or Application (Chrome) → Cookies → https://claude.ai. Copy the value of sessionKey and paste it into Settings → AIs → Claude. The key is stored in your Keychain. Anthropic doesn't officially support third-party apps reading Claude usage, so connecting Claude is at your own risk. Signing out of claude.ai in that browser may end the session, and then you'll need to paste a new key.

Connecting ChatGPT · Codex

Sign in to OpenAI's Codex app with your ChatGPT account first. Then, in Settings → AIs → ChatGPT · Codex, click Allow Access… and choose the hidden .codex folder in your home folder. Press ⌘⇧. in the file picker to show hidden folders.

Trying it without an account

Settings → General → Show sample data fills the widget with example numbers.

Common questions

What does the tick on each bar mean?

It marks how much of the window has passed. If the coloured fill is past the tick, you're using the limit faster than the window allows and will run out before it resets.

The numbers look out of date

The widget refreshes every 2 minutes (change this in Settings → General) and when your Mac wakes. A clock symbol means the last refresh failed. Check the message under that AI in Settings.
