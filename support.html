Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Open an issue and you'll get a reply there. Please don't paste session keys or other sign-in details into an issue.

Getting started

Where's the app?

On a Mac, AI Limits lives in the menu bar (look for the percentages) and as a widget on your desktop. Click the menu bar item for Settings, or right-click the widget. On Windows it sits in the notification area; on iPhone, iPad and Android, open the app and add its widget to your home screen.

Signing in to your AIs

Open Settings → AIs and click Sign In… next to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini (on iPhone, iPad and Android: Settings in the app). The AI's own sign-in page opens inside AI Limits. Sign in as you normally would, and the window closes by itself when you're done. The widget fills in within a few seconds.

Your sign-in stays in AI Limits' own browser storage, separate from your normal browser, so signing out of the app never signs you out anywhere else. Anthropic, OpenAI and Google don't officially support third-party apps reading usage, so connecting an AI is at your own risk.

Signing in with Google doesn't work

Google sometimes refuses sign-ins inside apps. If that happens, sign in to that AI with its email option instead of "Continue with Google", where it offers one. For Gemini there's no alternative yet, so please open an issue to let us know.

Other ways to connect (Mac and Windows)

If you use OpenAI's Codex app or Claude Code, AI Limits can read their sign-ins for an AI you haven't signed in to inside the app. On a Mac you can also paste a claude.ai sessionKey under Settings → AIs → Claude → Other ways to connect.

Trying it without an account

Settings → General → Show sample data fills the widget with example numbers.

Common questions

What does the tick on each bar mean?

It marks how much of the window has passed. If the coloured fill is past the tick, you're using the limit faster than the window allows and will run out before it resets.

The numbers look out of date

The widget refreshes every 2 minutes (change this in Settings → General) and when your Mac wakes. A clock symbol means the last refresh failed. Check the message under that AI in Settings.
